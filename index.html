The Wand Shop

Step up to the magic mirror, pick a wand from the table and wave it. Only the right movement will make a wand choose its wizard…

Stand back so your whole arm and wand are visible, and keep the room well lit.

Grown-up notes
  • How it works: the mirror follows whatever is moving (no coloured tip needed). The wand chooses the child when they trace the secret shape (a figure 8 or a zigzag); any other movement makes it fizzle.
  • Keep the camera still. Prop the phone up. A moving camera looks like moving everything.
  • Hidden controls: triple-tap the bottom-left corner of the screen (or press A) to choose the secret shape, re-roll for the next child, secretly force a result, pick the magic style, switch camera and tune sensitivity.
  • On an iPhone: turn the ring/silent switch off, turn the volume up and use landscape. Screen-mirror (AirPlay) to the TV. Add to Home Screen for full screen.
  • Keyboard: Space always reject, G always choose, N new wizard, ↑/↓ sensitivity, D debug view, H hide status dot, M mouse wand (draw with the mouse; keys 1, 2, 3 pick magic style), F full screen.
