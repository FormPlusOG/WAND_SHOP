The Wand Shop

Step up to the magic mirror, pick a wand from the table, and see if the wand chooses you.

Each wand has a brightly coloured tip (red, blue or green). Good lighting helps the mirror spot it.

Grown-up notes
  • Hidden controls: triple-tap the bottom-left corner of the screen (or press A) to open the grown-up panel: choose which wand colour is "the one", re-roll for the next child, force a rejection, switch camera, tune sensitivity and calibrate the tape colours.
  • On an iPhone: turn the ring/silent switch off, turn the volume up and use landscape. Screen-mirror or AirPlay to the TV. Add to Home Screen for a full-screen view.
  • Keys on a keyboard: Space force reject, N new wizard, ↑/↓ sensitivity, D debug view, H hide status dot, M mouse wand (keys 1, 2, 3 choose colour), F full screen.
